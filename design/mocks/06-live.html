📴 Offline — check-ins will sync 1 queued
Banner triggers on navigator.onLine + a failed heartbeat (airplane mode alone isn't reliable). The screen stays fully usable: reads come from the local SWR cache, writes go to the outbox. No feature is disabled offline — check-in is THE core offline action (temples have no wifi).
⚠️ 1 photo didn't upload. All check-ins themselves are saved.
Don't catastrophize: a single failed asset renders as a quiet toast + a badge on the one affected item — never a full-screen error. Auto-retry with exponential backoff (×5), then manual retry. The check-in row and the photo are separate sync units, so a fat photo can never block the check-in itself.
