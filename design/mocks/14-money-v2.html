#38 — the ledger sits last, and starts short. Twenty rows, then "load more" walking backwards through the months.

But it will not save you any data, and it is worth knowing why. The ledger is a jsonb column on the trip row (01-document-sync.sql:7), and TRIP_COLS (queries.ts:8) selects it whole in the same request as the trip state. Every byte has already arrived before this card renders — and the chart, the donut, the burn rate and the projection all need the complete array anyway. Truncating here buys render cost and scroll length, not bandwidth. Real pagination would mean moving the ledger out of the trip document into its own table: a migration, new RPCs, and the end of the offline-friendly whole-document sync. Probably right eventually; definitely not what "show fewer rows" costs.

Why the split is on this seam
Spending

Past tense. The tracker. Overview, chart, donut, ledger. Opened daily, on a phone, usually right after paying for something.

Plan

Future tense. The commitments. Bookings, subscriptions, one-offs, per-stop forecast, monthly, cap. Opened weekly, while planning.

The old split was Budget / Monthly / Ledger — cut by data type, which is why it got merged: you needed all three to answer one question. This cut is by tense, and the halves are used at different moments. That is the test the old tabs failed.

Recommendation: ship the one-page fix first — the #38 one-liner plus the two new cards. Nothing is wasted if we split later: the cards already sit in two contiguous blocks in the right order, so the segmented control becomes a small change on top.
