This is the honest fallback and it is not bad. It closes #35, #38 and #39 with no routing work. What it does not fix: ten cards is a long scroll on a phone, and the page still answers two unrelated questions at once — "what did we spend" and "what is still coming".
Why the split is along this seam
Spending

Past tense. The tracker. Overview, chart, donut, ledger. Opened daily, on a phone, usually right after paying for something.

Plan

Future tense. The commitments. Bookings, subscriptions, one-offs, per-stop forecast, monthly, cap. Opened weekly, while planning.

The old split was Budget / Monthly / Ledger — three tabs cut by data type, which is why it got merged: you needed all three to answer one question. This cut is by tense, and the two halves are genuinely used at different moments. That is the test the old tabs failed.

Recommendation: ship the one-page fix first — it is the #38 one-liner plus the two new cards, and nothing is wasted if we split later. Live with ten cards for a stop or two. If the scroll still annoys, the segmented control is a small change on top, because by then the cards already sit in two contiguous blocks in exactly the right order.
