Followers see
  • Route, cities & dates (day precision)
  • Last check-in city + "last seen" time
  • Check-ins, place ratings & comments marked public
  • Photos marked public
Followers never see
  • Money — budget, ledger, prices, anything with a currency
  • Private notes & booking references
  • Exact GPS / addresses (city-level only)
  • Documents, passports, insurance
Sanitization is enforced server-side: the /follow endpoint reads from a dedicated view that simply has no money/notes/ref columns — it is not client-side filtering. The same rules are shown to followers themselves on their page footer ("what you can see").
"Create follow link" opens a small modal: label (e.g. "Family"), optional expiry date, done → link + QR shown for easy WhatsApp sharing. Revoke is instant and permanent (token deleted; follower device shows the "revoked" state from mock 07). Open question: should links default to an expiry (trip end + 30 days)? Mock assumption: yes, editable.
