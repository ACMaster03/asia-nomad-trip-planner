My read
· The root problem was never styling — it's that Settings doesn't belong among content tabs. Any option that keeps five equal items inherits the awkwardness.
· 1d solves the structure AND the anchor problem: the capsule gives the row a spine, four labels breathe, and the gear is present but apart. Yes, it brings the gear back — but the confusion you disliked came from the gear sitting next to "+ Add" in the content area; in the nav row, right of the capsule, its scope reads clearly.
· If you want zero gear anywhere: 1a is the runner-up, accepting the five-segment squeeze.
· 1b and 1c are moods — friendly vs. literary. Both worth stealing from later (1b's icons could join 1d's capsule if labels ever feel dry).
