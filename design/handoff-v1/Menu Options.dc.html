Four ways to hold the bottom bar
My read on the current bar: it's the only surface in the app with no visual voice — five text labels, no icons, no landscape DNA. Patrik is right. Below, four directions on the same Home screen stub. My honest ranking is in each caption; 1b is my pick.
1a Icons + labels, classic
The safe fix: Lucide icons over the labels, hunter for active. Zero learning curve, instantly better than today. The "boring but right" option.

1b Active pill — my pick
Icons only, and the active tab blooms into a honeydew pill with its label. Calm when idle, expressive on touch — the same "quiet until it matters" grammar as the landscape. Modern without being trendy-fragile.
Home
1c Floating dock
A detached frosted capsule floating over content. Gorgeous over the valley and map screens; the cost is ~20px of content height and it can feel "designed" rather than native. Best if the map becomes central.
1d Raised check-in
Four tabs + a raised mauve check-in button in the center — the memory-making act gets the throne. Strong idea *if* check-ins are truly the daily habit; but it demotes Map to the More sheet, and mid-trip the map matters. Ask Patrik which act is really #1.

My insights, condensed
· Whatever wins, icons are non-negotiable — a travel app's nav should be scannable with a tired thumb on a night bus.
· 1b fits the brand grammar best: quiet until it matters, like the landscape washes. It also handles dark mode gracefully (pill goes deep sage).
· 1c is the prettiest screenshot but the weakest workhorse — frosted docks age fast and steal content height from dense screens like Money.
· 1d is a product decision disguised as a design one: it declares check-ins the app's center. Decide that with Patrik first, not in CSS.
· The five destinations themselves (Home · Trip · Money · Map · More) are right — I wouldn't reshuffle them, only re-dress them.
· Update: 1e retires More entirely — Trip settings behind a gear on Trip, Account as the Home avatar, Explore promoted to the bar. My new pick: 1e structure with 1b dressing.
· Final form: 1f — 1e + 1d's button as a Live-phase floating check-in. Everything survives: complete nav, the mauve moment, the phase-aware grammar.
· Final-final: 1g — Explore folds into Map (search icon, price pins), the bar drops to four, and the center button returns phase-aware: "Check in" while Live, "+ Add stop" while planning.
