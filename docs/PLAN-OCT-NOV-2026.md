# The plan to 30 November 2026

Written 2026-09-28 at the end of the positioning session. The goal it serves,
the ICP, the copy and the channel reasoning are in `docs/MARKETING.md`; the
interview script is `docs/INTERVIEW-GUIDE.md`. This file is the calendar and
the two weekly routines. The same routines, milestones and log exist as a
shared page with ticks that both owners can use on a phone:
https://claude.ai/artifact/UY8bxQ5ebK2Tvtq7WiRqs4 (private; share it from
its Share menu). When the plan changes, change it here first.

**The goal.** Strangers first get the link on **Saturday 31 October**. By
**Monday 30 November**: eight unassisted journeys by eight or more strangers,
each with a follower, eight to sixteen followers opening weekly, every
traveller checking in weekly.

**The rule that protects the goal.** Before 31 October, an interview beats an
hour of content, every time. Content compounds later; conversations deliver
the eight.

About three hours plus the dinner evening, beside development. Reddit moved
to Petra on 2026-09-28; Patrik's plate is the fuller one.

| When | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Run the goal numbers (journeys by strangers, followers active in the last 7 days, travellers who checked in in the last 7 days). Write them in the log below. | 30 min |
| Mon · Wed · Fri | Build-in-public post on Threads or X: one screenshot, three lines, what got built. | 3 × 15 min |

| Sunday | The review, together (below). | 30 min |

Development priorities, in order, and nothing else before 31 October. All
of them sit in the GitHub milestone **Pre-freeze check (22 Oct 2026)** with
the `high priority` label; they are checked together on Thursday 22 October,
one week before the freeze.

1. **Make the goal countable** (#120). Follower opens must be logged, or the
   second metric cannot be checked; a source per account, and one query via
   `tools/db.sh` that prints the three numbers. Week one.
2. **The follower stays logged in on the web** (#121). Granny's bug. Week one
   or two.
3. **The MVP path, end to end, unassisted** (#122): sign up → name the
   journey → three stops with dates → arcs and a nightly estimate on the globe
   → invite a partner → send the follow link → the follower sees the route.
   Then check in, log a spend, see the pace. Audit it with a fresh account in
   week two; fix what the watching tests find in weeks three and four.
4. **Copy diet on the path screens only** (#65): one line under a control,
   the rest behind an info tap.
5. **Privacy policy and Play data safety** (#123): the new usage data has to
   be declared before it ships; #5's unset company facts become due with the
   launch.
6. **iOS closed beta** (#124) to interviewees who asked for it, week four.
7. **Prove the follow link and digest reach Russia** (#125), with Artur and
   Angelina's family.
8. **Feature freeze on 31 October.** After that, only fixes for things that
   stopped one of the eight. The follower's own door (#126) and anything else
   from the growth loop waits for December.

Reddit warm-up, weeks one and two only: comment, never post, never link. A
new account that posts a project gets removed and the account flagged. From
week three, one post a month at most, as a person telling a story.

One-offs, week one: the three-field form on journey.livhold.com ("Leaving for
a month or more? When, how long, how do you plan today?"), the Google Search
Console check, the blog section on the site, and a one-page press kit (who we
are, the route so far, the September numbers, five photos, the dinner
sentence, a contact address).

## Both — the Sunday review, thirty minutes

1. The three numbers, and the interview count for the week.
2. Every place a stranger stopped this week, from the watching tests and the
   five-line notes. Pick the one to fix.
3. Words people used that are better than ours. Update the copy kit.
4. Next week's dinner list and the two Reddit posters to follow up.

## Week by week

**Week 1 · 28 Sep – 4 Oct · move to Hanoi (Wed 30 Sep)**
- Artur and Angelina on a video call: the first **watching test**, not a demo.
  They share their screen and build a journey; Patrik says nothing until they
  are done or stuck for two minutes; then "what did you expect to happen
  here?" at each stop. They are testers one and two.
- Petra: form live, Search Console checked, blog section up, press kit drafted.
- Patrik: goal query written; follower login persistence started.
- First blog post: "What September in Bangkok cost us", numbers from Money.
- Reddit (Petra): comments only.

**Week 2 · 5 – 11 Oct**
- First dinner. Coworking scouted. Three interviews.
- Patrik's parents get the follow link on a call and are watched opening it:
  follower tests three and four. Petra's mother shows what broke in her
  domestic journey, screen shared (#122).
- First shorts published (the September numbers as the hook).
- Patrik: MVP path audited with a fresh account; login persistence done.
- Petra: start editing the Bangkok month video.

**Week 3 · 12 – 18 Oct**
- Dinner. Three interviews. Watching tests two and three, from the building.
- Bangkok month video published. Hungarian group posts start, with the video.
- Patrik: fixes from watching tests.

**Week 4 · 19 – 25 Oct**
- Dinner. Three interviews. Watching tests four and five.
- iOS closed beta to interviewees who asked. Copy diet on the path screens.
- **Thu 22 Oct: pre-freeze check** of every issue in the milestone, together.
- Petra: press list final (the verified base is in MARKETING.md, Hungarian
  channels), pitch email drafted, kit updated with October photos.

**Week 5 · 26 Oct – 1 Nov · launch to strangers**
- **Sat 31 Oct: feature freeze.** The link goes to everyone interviewed who
  committed, and to the landing-page list.
- The dinner is the launch dinner.
- Press pitches go out on Mon 2 Nov, once the app is public.

**Week 6 · 2 – 8 Nov** — target: three journeys started by strangers. Dinner.
Interviews continue; the commitment ask is now "set up your journey while I
watch". October (Hanoi, month one) cost post and shorts.

**Week 7 · 9 – 15 Nov** — target: five. Message every traveller whose
follower has not opened the feed; ask what the follower said. Press
follow-ups. Hanoi month video.

**Week 8 · 16 – 22 Nov** — target: seven. Anyone silent for seven days gets a
personal message from Patrik or Petra, never an automated one.

**Week 9 · 23 – 29 Nov** — target: eight. Write the November review: what the
eight did, where they stopped, what they said.

**Mon 30 Nov — goal check.** The result goes into `docs/NOTES.md` and the
goal section of `docs/MARKETING.md`, hit or missed, with the numbers.
