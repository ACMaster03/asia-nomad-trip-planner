# What people say they need

One entry per thing people want, or hate, in a travel app. Written in their words. The
number of an entry never changes, so sources and takeaways can point at it.

Count sources, not comments: five people in one thread is one source. When a new source
repeats a need, add its ID and quote under the existing entry; do not open a new one.

Jobs: `plan` · `money` · `follow` · `diary` · `other`.

## Shape of an entry (copy it)
