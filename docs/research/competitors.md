## TripIt

- What it is, as people describe it: an app that builds your trip from the booking confirmation emails you forward to it. Free, with a paid version.
- Who uses it, from the threads: people who travel often; one planning a multi-leg trip (S-01, r/femaletravelers).
- Praised for:
  - "Works flawlessly , free , used it for years" — S-01 [4]
  - "Forward your confirmation emails to plans@tripit.com and it builds your trips for you and can sync to your local calendar." — S-01 [5]
  - "Also nice to see all the old trip/memories in one app" — S-01 [3]
  - "really came through for me when I was planning a multi-leg trip" — S-01
- Complained about:
  - "except when there might be adjustments to travel plans it can get a bit confusing" — S-01 [3]
- Price people mention: free and paid versions; a "big Black Friday offer" on the paid one; one person would pay full price "for the next time I have a lot going on" — S-01
- Sources: S-01
