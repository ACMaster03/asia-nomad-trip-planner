### Round 1 — 2 Oct 2026, Claude's draft from S-01 only. Patrik decides.

One thread, about 80 comments, r/femaletravelers, on how people keep their bookings in
order. One source is one source; nothing below is settled.

- **New:** most people use no travel app at all. Email folders, a calendar, a document or
  a spreadsheet, or paper, usually two of them at once (S-01, the table "How many use
  what"). The two apps named, TripIt and Wanderlog, win on one thing: forward the booking
  email and the trip builds itself (N-01). Typing bookings into an app by hand is the thing
  people refuse to do, and one commenter says so outright. Livhold's planner asks for
  exactly that typing.
- **Repeats inside the thread, unprompted:** it has to work with no internet (N-06, six
  upvotes and four more); not being locked into one app, with a backup for a lost phone
  (N-07); a simple trip gets the phone or the calendar, a complex one gets the heavier
  tool (N-05). The confirmation number, address and phone must be there the moment they
  are needed (N-09); Livhold has no field for them.
- **Where Livhold already stands:** the trip at a glance with nights per stop (N-08),
  booked versus estimate (N-10), the diary (N-12), and any device with an export (N-07).
  None of those was asked for by name in this thread. The thread is about keeping
  bookings, not about following along; the follow idea did not come up.
- **For Patrik:** (1) Is importing bookings, from a forwarded email or a pasted screenshot,
  a direction for Livhold or not? Every other finding here is small next to it. (2) This
  subreddit is wary of apps: one commenter assumed the question was an app promotion.
  Worth knowing before anyone posts there.
