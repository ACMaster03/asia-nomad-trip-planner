## Needs heard

- N-01 — The trip builds itself from the booking emails or screenshots; no typing.
- N-02 — The trip shows up in the phone's calendar.
- N-03 — Changing plans mid-way should not be confusing.
- N-04 — Old trips and their memories stay in one place.
- N-05 — Free for a simple trip; worth paying when a lot is going on.
- N-06 — It works without internet.
- N-07 — Not locked into one app: every device, a backup, a lost phone survived.
- N-08 — The whole trip at a glance, in one place: days per place, rest days.
- N-09 — The confirmation number, address and phone at hand the moment they are needed.
- N-10 — Mark what is booked and what is still a maybe.
- N-11 — Flight changes come to me: terminal, gate, delays.
- N-12 — A journal inside the planner.
- N-13 — Small daily notes next to the plan: laundry, food for tomorrow, cash owed.
