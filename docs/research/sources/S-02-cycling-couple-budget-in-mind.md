## Who

The poster, from the post itself: a couple, left Portugal by bicycle in May 2025, 15
countries across Europe and Asia, now in South Korea, Japan next, then Southeast Asia.
Both work online teaching languages; laptops travel in the panniers. They share the
journey publicly on Instagram. No usernames kept here.

## The question (Petra's comment)

> "My partner and I just started travelling while working, getting a taste of this new
> lifestyle, and it's hard to keep track of our plans and such. So may I ask how do you
> keep track of your expenses, and plan where to go next?"

## What they said

Word for word, from the poster's reply.

### Money

> "Honestly, we don't really keep detailed expense records. We simply try not to spend
> much and always keep our budget in mind. We know how much we earn and have a pretty
> clear idea of how much we can afford to spend."

> "We camp a lot, stay in cheap hotels when needed, and cook most of the time, depending
> on the country we're in. Travelling by bicycle also saves us a lot on transportation."

> "For us, the key is simply keeping our expenses low and staying flexible."

### Planning the next place

> "As for where we go next, we're very flexible. We usually look at the weather and
> whether the next destination is realistically possible and makes sense for us. We have
> a general direction, but we don't plan everything in advance."

### Work and stays

> "When we need to work, we usually rent an apartment for 2–3 days, get some good Wi-Fi,
> teach our classes, and then continue."

From the post: "We usually spend a few days in a place when we need good Wi-Fi and time
to work, then get back on our bikes and continue."

### Why they travel

> "We want to see as much of the world as we can, and travelling this way makes it
> possible for us."

From the post: "being digital nomads isn't really about working from cafés in beautiful
places. It's about having the freedom to choose where we live and travel".

### They asked back

> "What about you guys? How are you travelling, and what kind of work do you do?"
